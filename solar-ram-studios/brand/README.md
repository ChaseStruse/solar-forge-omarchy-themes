# Solar Ram Studios wallpaper theme

The approved direction combines a powerful charcoal ram, sculpted flowing fur, curled ridged horns, emerald illumination, and cool silver-white highlights. The mood is cinematic dark fantasy with selective futuristic architecture and energy effects.

## Consistency rules

- Let charcoal and black dominate. Use emerald to guide attention and silver-white for crisp highlights.
- Preserve the ram’s strong facial identity, flowing forehead texture and substantial curled horns.
- Keep horns and fur free of runes, glyphs and decorative lettering.
- Use emerald eyes and rim lighting. Avoid gold, yellow and orange lighting in this theme.
- Combine tactile fantasy detail with restrained energy effects or futuristic structures.
- Keep part of the desktop quiet and dark for icons; maintain breathing room around the ram and wordmark.
- The approved logo uses cinematic Roman serif lettering, with “SOLAR RAM” above smaller “STUDIOS.” Lettering in these PNGs is image-generated, not a confirmed installed font. Do not assume Zen Dots is part of this Solar Ram direction.

## Technical details

All three wallpapers are RGB PNG files at **1672 × 941 pixels**, approximately 16:9. They are native generated assets, not native 4K. A desktop may scale them to fit its display; larger screens can show softer detail. “Fit” preserves the whole composition; “Fill” can crop edges on a display with a different aspect ratio. Keep original files when creating additional sizes.

The logo reference is a separate square asset. PNG artwork is raster, not editable vector artwork. Use sRGB as the working color space for new screen assets; embedded profile metadata has not been verified.
